What the work connects

The business behind the website.
In a form an assistant can use.

Identity and offer

Your name, brokerage relationship, market coverage, relevant credentials and services should agree across the website and connected profiles. The visible site stays the primary explanation for customers.

Evidence and context

Connect work examples, reviews, credentials and useful local writing to their sources. Clear ownership and dates make information easier to check and maintain. Unsupported claims do not become evidence just because they appear in structured data.

Requests and results

Define the request, required details and customer permission before connecting an action. The response should distinguish an inquiry received from an appointment confirmed, and hand off to a person wherever judgment is required.

Structured data, accessible page content and optional discovery files can help publish these details. They are not a universal agent interface and cannot guarantee that a particular assistant will read, cite or recommend you.

1. Review the current picture

Check the website, important profiles, services, evidence and inquiry path. Identify contradictions and missing context, rather than assume the whole platform must be replaced.

2. Build and check the agreed foundation

Implement the client-owned website, connected business information and supported discovery surfaces. Check mobile usability, accessibility, metadata, links and the agreed form behavior.

3. Connect an action if it fits

For a separately scoped action, test valid and invalid requests, repeated submissions and the actual outcome. An assistant should never tell a customer that an appointment exists because an email was submitted.

4. Hand over the asset

Transfer the site and accounts, explain how to maintain the facts, and make ongoing service responsibilities clear.
